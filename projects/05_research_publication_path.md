# Research & Publication Path

The course ends with research capability, not merely a working model.

## Research maturity ladder

**Reproduce → Measure → Break → Explain → Intervene → Ablate → Generalize → Communicate → Release**

Every paper-oriented project should contain a precise research question and falsifiable hypothesis; related work and accurate citations; dataset provenance, split and licensing/access basis; method details; a predeclared evaluation protocol; baseline/intervention/ablation results; repeated seeds where feasible; figures; error analysis; resource accounting; limitations; a reproducibility manifest; code/configuration and dataset-access instructions; and follow-up experiments.

Before submission, verify that comparisons are fair, budgets are comparable, the baseline is credible, the metric answers the question, and claims are no broader than the evidence.

The course progression is: **neural-computing reproduction → controlled architecture experiment → deep-learning ablation → Transformer/LLM reproduction → data/training intervention → evaluation/failure analysis → final paper or technical report.**
