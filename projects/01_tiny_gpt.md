# Project 1 — Tiny GPT From Scratch

## Goal

Implement enough of a decoder-only language model to understand every major tensor operation.

## Required components

- tokenizer;
- embedding;
- positional mechanism;
- causal attention;
- multi-head attention;
- MLP;
- residual/normalization;
- output projection;
- cross-entropy;
- optimizer;
- generation;
- checkpointing.

## Evidence

A learning curve, generated samples, tensor-shape audit, parameter count, and failure analysis.

## Extension

Compare two architectures or tokenizer choices and write a short research note.
