# Course 1 · Chapter — Deep Reinforcement Learning

**Lab:** [Open the executable laboratory](./lab.ipynb)

Learn Markov decision processes, value functions, policy learning, temporal-difference learning, deep Q-networks, replay buffers, tar\\get networks and evaluation under stochasticity.

## Practical laboratory

Use Gymnasium CartPole-v1. Implement DQN with a replay buffer and tar\\get network. Evaluate at least 20 episodes per condition.

## Research exercise

Ablate replay and tar\\get networks separately. Report mean return, variance, learning curves, sample efficiency and runtime. Explain failures rather than selecting only the best run.

## Paper-ready evidence

Produce a comparison table, learning-curve figure, failure analysis, resource record and a reproducible configuration.
