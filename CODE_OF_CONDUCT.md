# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making this a welcoming, respectful, and rigorous educational and research community.

Participants are expected to:

- treat contributors with respect;
- criticize ideas and evidence rather than people;
- welcome questions from learners at different levels;
- distinguish uncertainty from error and disagreement from disrespect;
- give appropriate credit to prior work;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks.

Maintainers may moderate contributions and discussions to preserve a constructive learning and research environment.

For security-sensitive reports, use the repository's security reporting mechanism rather than a public issue.
