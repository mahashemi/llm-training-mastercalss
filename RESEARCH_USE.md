# Research and Development Use

This repository is designed to be usable as a citable research and engineering resource.

## Use in research

You may use the repository to:

- learn methods;
- reproduce experiments;
- build derivative experiments;
- adapt notebooks to new datasets or models;
- compare training approaches;
- create educational material;
- develop technical proposals;
- use the documented methods in research and development work.

When publishing results derived from the repository, cite both this repository and the primary scientific sources relevant to the method.

## Cite the exact artifact

A citation should identify what was actually used:

- release/tag;
- commit;
- notebook path;
- textbook chapter;
- runbook;
- dataset or model identifier.

## Do not overclaim

Material in this repository may include estimates, heuristics, teaching simplifications, experimental observations, or evolving recommendations. Reproduce and validate important claims before using them as evidence for a high-stakes engineering or scientific decision.

## Third-party material

This repository references external papers, videos, datasets, models, libraries, and other resources. Their licenses remain their own. Check the original license before redistributing third-party material.

## Responsible reproduction

Do not reproduce experiments using private, restricted, or unlawfully obtained data. Do not expose credentials or confidential information.

## Corrections

If you find an error that could affect research conclusions, open an issue describing the claim, source, evidence, and proposed correction.
